About visimo.ai

Understanding the technology behind facial emotion detection

🧠 How It Works

  1. Your webcam captures live video frames
  2. Face detection locates your face (Haar Cascade)
  3. The face region is preprocessed (CLAHE, resize, normalize)
  4. A CNN processes the face and outputs 7 emotion probabilities
  5. Results are displayed in real-time with confidence scores

🛡️ Vulnerability Safeguards

  • Privacy: Consent screen, no data stored, local processing
  • Bias Awareness: Confidence caps, disclaimers about limitations
  • Quality Checks: Lighting, blur, and distance monitoring
  • Transparency: All emotion scores shown, not just the top one

📊 Model Architecture

  • Type: Convolutional Neural Network (CNN)
  • Layers: 3 Conv blocks + BatchNorm + Dropout
  • Parameters: 739,303
  • Input: 48×48 grayscale images
  • Training: FER2013 (28,709 images), 30 epochs
  • Augmentation: Rotation, flip, zoom, shift
